\section{Graph structure, hubness and robustness}
\label{sec:structure-protocol}

The structure of the graph each representation induces is measured on the
directed kNN graph with $k=10$, built inside the gallery (Section
\ref{sec:graph-gallery}), with near-duplicates collapsed. Every number below
is computed by \texttt{musicsim/evaluation/\{hubness, structure, communities\}.py}
and \texttt{musicsim/graphs/compare.py}.

\paragraph{Hubness.} The k-occurrence $N_k(i)$ is the number of nodes that list
$i$ among their $k$ neighbours, that is, the in-degree of $i$. In high dimension
it becomes right-skewed: a few \emph{hubs} appear in many neighbour lists and
many \emph{antihubs} in none. The headline summary is the skewness of $N_k$, the
population third standardised moment, defined as $0$ when $N_k$ is constant
(a directed ring, for instance). Secondary: the fraction of antihubs
($N_k=0$), the fraction of hubs, defined as $N_k > 2\,\overline{N_k}$, and the
largest $N_k$. The threshold uses the mean in-degree and not $2k$ so that it
stays meaningful when degrees vary; a threshold based on the standard deviation
is avoided because it grows with the skew it is meant to detect, and would hide
the hubs of the most biased representation. \todo{Cite Radovanovi\'c et al.\ (2010)
for the definition of hubness once the paper has been read and the entry moved to
the bibliography.}

\paragraph{Communities against genre.} Louvain communities are computed on the
undirected union graph with unit edge weights (the cosine weights of a kNN graph
can be non-positive, and Louvain requires positive ones), resolution 1, and 10
seeds. The seed of each run depends on the experiment seed and the run number and
never on the label of the representation, so two identical graphs receive
identical partitions. The headline is the normalised mutual information between
the partition and the genre labels (arithmetic-mean normalisation), averaged over
seeds. Reported beside it, because a partition that changes from one seed to the
next says little about the graph: the modularity of the partition and its
stability, the mean adjusted Rand index over all pairs of seeds. Isolated nodes
are communities of their own. \todo{Cite Blondel et al.\ (2008) once verified.}

\paragraph{Label homophily.} The fraction of directed edges whose two endpoints
share \texttt{genre\_top}. On a graph built without removing near-duplicates this
is exactly the mean precision at 10 of the retrieval evaluator (Section
\ref{sec:retrieval-protocol}) under the \texttt{raw} variant. It is therefore
reported as a structure metric and not as a second retrieval result, and the
reader should not count it as independent evidence. Its chance level is the
homophily of the random control, about $1/8$ with eight roughly balanced
genres, and the gain over that control is what is interpreted.

\paragraph{Other structure statistics.} Reciprocity (fraction of edges $i\to j$
whose reverse $j\to i$ exists); mean local clustering coefficient on the union
graph, averaged over all nodes with coefficient $0$ for degree below 2; the
number of weakly connected components and the fraction of nodes in the largest
one. These are secondary.

\paragraph{Neighbour-vote classification.} Each node is assigned the genre that
its neighbours vote for, a vote being the edge weight, with ties going to the
smallest genre code. The node's own artist never votes, since otherwise the
graph would reward recognising the artist and not the genre. A node with no
admissible neighbour abstains, which counts as an error, and the abstention rate
is reported. This is \emph{transductive}: the neighbours' labels come from the
same gallery as the node classified, so the figures describe the graph and not a
model. They are not comparable with the inductive classifiers of the classical
block and are never placed in the same table as them.

\paragraph{Comparison between graphs.} For each pair of representations over the
same nodes: neighbour Jaccard@10 (headline), edge overlap, Spearman correlation
of the k-occurrences, and ARI between partitions (seed $i$ of one graph against
seed $i$ of the other, averaged). A statistic that is undefined, such as the
correlation with a constant k-occurrence, is \texttt{nan} and not an arbitrary
value. The pairs among the three draws of the random control give the floor of a
graph that carries no structure. It is \emph{not} the noise floor between seeds
of a trained model that the criterion for PQ requires; that one needs the trained
models and is measured later.

\paragraph{Intervals.} Structure is a property of a whole graph, not of a query,
so there is no per-query resampling. Instead, 200 subsamples are drawn, each
keeping 80\,\% of the artists of the gallery without replacement and all of their
tracks, and the graph of every representation is \emph{rebuilt} from the
surviving nodes, never cropped. The subsamples are the same for every
representation, which makes the differences between representations paired.
Inside a subsample Louvain runs with 3 seeds to bound the cost; the point
estimate keeps 10. Reported intervals are the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles over
the subsamples. This is a subsample without replacement, not a bootstrap: its
interval describes how much a metric moves when a fifth of the artists is left
out, it understates the variability of the full sampling, it need not contain the
point estimate (graphs of fewer nodes have different k-occurrence and Louvain
behaviour), and no p-value is derived from it. Differences are therefore read as
paired intervals that exclude or include 0, and are stated as descriptive.

\paragraph{Headline metrics and what they are independent of.} The three headline
structure metrics are the skewness of the k-occurrence, the NMI between Louvain
communities and genre, and label homophily. The last two are defined with the
genre labels, so they belong to the proxy-based family of conclusions and
inherit its circularity; the skewness of the k-occurrence is the only headline
structure metric that does not use any label, and the report says so wherever it
is read. All structure numbers on FMA belong to that family until the human
judgements of MagnaTagATune are available.

\section{Model selection}
\label{sec:model-selection-protocol}

Any decision that could be tuned to the test split is instead made on FMA
\emph{validation}: early stopping, hyper-parameter grids, and -- the first
case the code implements -- MERT's titular layer. Each of the 13 hidden
states \texttt{mert} exposes is L2-normalised and ranked by Recall@10 on
validation only (\texttt{musicsim.extractors.mert.select\_titular\_layer});
the winner is the layer every later stage uses, never re-chosen against test:
layer 6 (of 0--12), R@10 = 0.0548 on the 800-item validation gallery
(\todo{13-layer figure: Recall@10 against layer index}). The same rule governs every later
selection: F1's $\alpha$, F3's cut-offs, and the GNN's binary-versus-intensity
objective are all chosen on validation, stated here once rather than repeated
at each one.

\section{Invariants}
\label{sec:invariants}

The whole evaluation rests on three rules that the code enforces rather than
assumes. Rows and identifiers stay aligned, and every cross-reference goes
through the identifier, never through a position. Anything fitted, the scaler,
the PCA and the probes, is fitted on the training split alone. Every source of
randomness is seeded from the single seed the run records.
