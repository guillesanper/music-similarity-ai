\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\todo{Be explicit and do not soften any of these:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Statistical power: 860 constraints over 337 triplets give intervals of
        roughly $\pm 3$ to 5 points.
  \item Domain shift: MTT is Magnatune at 16\,kHz, FMA is 22\,050\,Hz.
  \item The genre labels are chosen by the artists who uploaded the music.
  \item Excerpts of 30 seconds are not whole tracks.
  \item Human agreement itself is far from unanimous, which caps any model.
\end{itemize}}
